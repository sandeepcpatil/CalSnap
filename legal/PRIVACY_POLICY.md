# CalVue — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

> This is a good-faith template, not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a lawyer and fill in the bracketed fields before publishing. Mirrors the in-app Privacy Policy (`mobile/src/content/legal.ts`).

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how CalVue collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

## 2. Information we collect
- **Account information:** your email address, name, and profile photo from your sign-in provider.
- **Health & profile data you provide:** weight, height, age, gender, activity level, and goals — used to calculate your targets.
- **Food data:** the photos you scan and the resulting food logs (name, calories, macros, meal type, time).
- **Subscription information:** your subscription status and history, via RevenueCat and the app stores. We do not receive or store your card details.
- Basic usage and device information needed to run and improve the App.

## 3. How we use your information
We use your information to: provide food analysis and tracking, calculate your nutrition targets, manage your subscription and trial, provide support, keep the App secure, and improve the service.

## 4. AI processing of your photos and voice clips
To estimate nutrition, the food images you scan are sent to our AI provider, Google (Gemini API), for analysis. If you describe a meal by voice, the clip is sent to Google Gemini to be transcribed. We send only what is needed for the estimate. Please review Google's applicable terms for how they handle API data. Images are deleted from our storage after 90 days.

## 5. How we share information
We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run CalVue: Supabase (secure hosting and authentication), Google (sign-in and AI analysis via Gemini), RevenueCat and Google Play (subscription billing), and Open Food Facts (packaged-food lookups, which receive the barcode only). We may disclose information if required by law.

## 6. What we keep and for how long

| Data | What it is used for | Kept for |
|---|---|---|
| Meal photos | Sent to Google Gemini to estimate your meal. | Deleted from our storage 90 days after upload. |
| Voice clips | Sent to Google Gemini to be transcribed into a meal entry. | Not stored by us after the entry is created. |
| Nutrition logs | Your daily totals, history, insights and export. | Until you delete the entry or your account. |
| Water and weight | Your hydration goal and weight trend. | Until you delete the entry or your account. |
| Profile (height, weight, age, activity level, goal) | Sets your daily targets. | Until you change it or delete your account. |
| Account (name, email and photo from Google sign-in) | Keeps your data tied to you. | Until you delete your account. |

Deleting your account in the App (Profile, then Delete account) removes the account, all logs and all photos. This cannot be undone.

## 7. Your rights & choices
You can access and correct your profile in the App. You can delete your account and all associated data in the App (Profile, then Delete account) or by emailing us at [calsnap.support@gmail.com]. Pro members can export their nutrition log as a spreadsheet from the App.

**Your rights.** You can ask us for access to the personal data we hold about you, for correction of anything inaccurate, for deletion of your data, or for a copy of your data in a portable form (the in-app spreadsheet export covers your nutrition log). To exercise any of these, email [calsnap.support@gmail.com] from the address on your account and we will respond within a reasonable time. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable data protection laws.

## 8. Security
We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

## 9. Children
CalVue is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. International transfers
Your information may be processed on servers located outside your country (for example, by our hosting and AI providers). We take steps to ensure appropriate protection for such transfers.

## 11. Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be notified in the App, and the "last updated" date above will change.

## 12. Contact
For privacy questions or requests, contact us at [calsnap.support@gmail.com].
